## Implemented boundaries

The R22 acquisition handoff remains one-use and preserves fresh complete classification. The exact A-bound claim and immutable owner cover session, wake, authority, capture, reservation and request identity. Under the existing queue lock the observer records the actual post-claim pending occupant, first and latest successor, replacement counts, freshness and origin (framework, controlled or synthetic); B-start and B-end are independent snapshots. The reader yields only for its own A token. Reduction stores a bounded versioned owner/ordinal tuple in the protected wake core; it tolerates late and out-of-order events, reports open/incomplete observation explicitly and preserves old captures. No new display authority, worker, delayed revocation or cached MAIN grant was added.

The real-service fixture binds controls to the original A reservation and independently inventories real SystemUI windows. Controlled and unstaged cases have separate results. A debug-only fixed-phase observer supports the calibrated visual references; debug bytecode contained the hook, while compiled profile and release code contained no hook reference. `KeyguardCardSettings`, `KeyguardOpeningInspectionHandoff`, `KeyguardWindowSnapshotReader`, `KeyguardCardInspectionQueue`, and `FluviKeyguardCardService` are the application-side changes; the remaining changes are test scaffolding and the test-only Robolectric dependency.
