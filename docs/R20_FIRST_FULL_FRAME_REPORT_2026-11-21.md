## Scoped implementation and ownership

Only the explicit `authorizeDirectFirst` ticket may choose the ticket-qualified FULL miss. It validates the existing size, exact material/disclosure/phase key, target, generation, authorization and current owner. An exact poster hit stays on the bitmap path. A miss constructs the current painter on the existing worker, stores the exact first phase, configures before native holder access, clears and draws the painter directly to the existing hidden hardware Surface; it creates no intermediate Picture/bitmap or cache entry. On an exact-current successful post it records the selected phase/visual metadata and notifies readiness once. Revoke, replacement and failure revoke the original authority and clear first-use state; no separate output, new worker or permission is introduced. Existing non-ticket, discreet, static, holder, normal update, UI geometry and admission/retirement paths are preserved.

The original ticket and one compact first-write summary are bound to the original session/wake/output/capture. They distinguish DIRECT_MISS and BITMAP_HIT and record entry, painter construction, configuration, lock, draw and successful post on the monotonic nanosecond clock. R19's uptime lifecycle timings, first physical read and `R19_PAINT_ENTER` remain distinct. Missing endpoints are explicit; no image, palette key or user data enters telemetry. Diagnostic failure or a frozen capture cannot grant or revoke rendering.

R1–R5 inspection: R1 scoped selection and hardware canvas are implemented, with real panel comparison required; R2 preserves exact-key hit/other routes and has separate scoped hit/retirement tests; R3 exact post/readiness and old/new owner safety are covered by held-lock/failure controls; R4 compact LIGHT/FULL export, freeze and original-ticket association have pressure tests; R5 canonical hardware View, actual panel, and real-service controls are independent. Code inspection and compiled tests do not replace their execution.
