## Root-cause finding

`_selectedMindSumYear` currently drives both the animated Stage1 rail and the selected-year Stage2 frame. Every crossed tick calls `setState`, which rebuilds a full `StatsRenderFrame` for the new year and reconstructs the 12-month heatmap while the drag animation is active. The Stage2 grid uses `constraints.maxWidth` rather than a single seven-column cell width when calculating `childAspectRatio`, producing cells about one seventh of their intended height.
