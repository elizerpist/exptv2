## Geometry and material

`FluviWaveShell` is one chart-local parametric surface, not another financial
series or independent landscape. At section depth zero its position equals
the genuine bounded financial ridge. Through depth it moves right/down,
foreshortens across the plot, rolls forward and returns inward. The final
rounded turn uses `sin²(πv)`, sweep `sin(πv/2)`, height-turn/return coefficients
`.70/.14`, camera shear `.08`, and depth `min(52, .17 × chartWidth)`. The
foreground retains ordered, distinct financial relief; the returned edge
retains 86% of the ridge's height variation. This is not a common flat floor.

The declared financial baseline is `.62` of the internal plot height. Only
internal safe padding changes; uniform calendar X spacing and linear exact
minor-unit Y scaling remain authoritative. The card allocation does not move.
Surface vertices, contours, inner return and normals share one geometry owner.
Normals are derivatives of that actual projected surface with foreshortened
physical depth, not a same-screen-X height-field approximation.

Fallback vertex colors and shader uniforms share one material policy: lavender
body, thin pearl financial rim, pale recessed return, broad soft normal-based
lighting and a final grazing highlight at the turning silhouette. No broad
translucent layer is used to hide geometry or create route-dependent overlap.
The only fade is the terminal lip. The shader reads a cached two-dimensional
normal/depth/coverage atlas rasterized from the same triangles; it no longer
reconstructs depth between ridge/foot at equal screen X. Its native fallback
always paints the same shell and exact financial data.

Deleted independent atmosphere profiles/meshes and `_ShaderAtmosphereLayer`/
Aurora work, including its continuous animation owner. The historical persisted
`shaderAtmosphere` identifier remains compatible but renders no atmosphere.
Zero months paint neither invented hills nor a financial marker. All three
wave presets share this corrected surface. Original Current is unchanged.
